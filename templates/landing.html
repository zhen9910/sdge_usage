How to get started
1
Click Install Chrome Extension above and add it to Chrome.
2
Click the extension icon in your toolbar and press Connect SDGE Account. The SDGE login page opens.
3
Log in to SDGE. The extension detects your login automatically — come back here and your dashboard will be ready.

Your SDGE credentials are never stored on this server. Session data is encrypted and tied only to your browser.
